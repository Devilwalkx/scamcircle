# Screen decisions

For each screen: why the person opened it, what they see first, the primary action, what happens next, and what happens when information or connectivity is missing. Written for the Android pass; the app is a test build and none of this has been tried by a fresh user (see USABILITY_SESSION_PLAN.md, result NOT RUN).

Shell: top bar with the section name, a Back button (not on Home) and Settings; a bottom bar with three tabs, **People - Check - Requests**, the Check tab in the centre and emphasised. System Back unwinds one step inside a screen, then goes to Home, then leaves the app. Tapping the tab you are on returns to its first screen. The tab bar hides while the keyboard is open.

| Screen | Why opened | First information | Primary action | What happens next | If something is missing |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Home** (Check tab) | "I have a message I am not sure about." | "Not sure about a message?" and one big centred button. A chip says it works without internet. | **Check a message** | Opens the check screen. Recent checks, Trusted people and Requests sit below, quieter. | First run (no checks): a quiet "See a made-up example" adds ONE clearly marked example message (no helper, no trusted number) that can be deleted. No explanatory tour first. A one-line "test build" notice is the only caveat on this screen. |
| **Check a message** | To put the message in. | One box, plus Paste from clipboard and "It is in a picture". | **Check this message** (disabled until there is text) | Result screen. The unfinished text is kept sealed on the phone and cleared when checked or cleared. | Text shared from another app arrives pre-filled with a note saying so. A picture: shown as "not analysed", with the option to crop it for sharing; the person types what it says. No camera or storage permission is asked for. |
| **Result** ("What we found") | To learn what was noticed and what to do. | One sentence that says what was and was not done ("We found 3 warning signs in the words. Who sent it is still not verified."), then four separate cards: what we found; what we could not check; who sent it (Not verified); settle it by checking another way. | **I checked it another way**, or **Ask someone I trust to look** | Record a check, or the ask flow. Evidence (the message with the warning words marked) is one tap away. | Inconclusive (not English, unreadable, too short): the same four cards appear and say it tells you nothing either way, and still give the independent check and the "look the number up yourself from an official source" advice. No screen says safe, genuine or fine to pay. |
| **How did you check?** | To record a check made outside the app. | Ways that count, then ways that do not (they can still be recorded). | **Save** | Says whether it counted. Only an independent route with a clear answer changes "Not verified" to "Checked by you another way". | A number or route added after the message arrived does not count, and says why. |
| **Ask someone I trust** | To get a second pair of eyes. Voluntary and granular. | Two ways: plain text through my own messaging app, or a private code for a checked person. | Next, through: (private code only) who; what they see (hide phone numbers, bank details, any word); what else (note, warning-sign names, expiry); a review step | Text route: a "message to share" and the share sheet. Code route: a private code and the share sheet. | Both end on a card that says it is not sent, and that ScamCircle cannot tell whether it arrived or was answered (no delivery receipt, no reply notification). No checked people yet: says so and links to People. The pretend same-phone helper appears only if created in Demonstration tools and is labelled "Not a real person". |
| **Crop a picture to share** | To hide parts of a picture before sending it to someone. | "This picture was not analysed", a preview with dark bands for what is cut off, trim buttons by edge (no dragging needed). | **Share the cropped picture** | Opens the system share sheet with the trimmed copy. | Not encrypted by ScamCircle and says so. In a browser sharing is not available and it says so. |
| **People** | To add or check someone who can look at a message. | A warning that nothing is automatic (no server, no receipts), the people list, my code words. | **Add a person** | Swap contact codes, then read code words out to each other. | Empty state explains the steps. A changed code shows a warning card and blocks sharing until checked again. |
| **Requests** | Someone asked me to look at a message. | One box for a code they sent me, then the list. | **Open the code** | The request opens with the redacted message, their note, and "do not use the number or link in the message". | Nothing waiting: an empty state. Expired and stopped requests say so. |
| **Settings** | Change display, read what the app cannot do, manage data, check the build. | Display (colours, text size), privacy and limits, your data, About this build. | n/a | Sub-pages: Works without internet, How well does the checker work, What this app cannot do, backup, restore, erase. | Erase asks for confirmation and states what cannot be undone. Demonstration tools are last, collapsed, labelled "not for real use". |
| **Works without internet** | "Why does it say no internet?" | What works offline, why there is no internet permission, what that means. | n/a | n/a | States it is a design choice, not a fault, and what is not tested. |
| **How well does the checker work?** | To judge how far to trust it. | "We do not know how well it protects real people", then counts from three small made-up test sets, read live from the code. | n/a | n/a | Says plainly that a message avoiding the wording patterns gets no warning signs. |
| **Demonstration tools** | Trying the app on one phone. | A loud strip: DEMONSTRATION ONLY, a simulation. | Add a pretend second person / load the example story | Pretend helper side and pretend clock. | Never shown in ordinary use. |

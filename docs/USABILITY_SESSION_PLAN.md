# Usability session plan (RESULTS: NOT RUN)

No fresh users were available for this pass. Nothing in this repository is a usability result. The author's own walk-through of the app does not count and is not recorded as one.

## Who
Five or more adults who did not build or see the app, including at least two aged 65+, at least one who uses large system text, at least one who has been targeted by a scam message (self-reported), and at least one TalkBack user. Real consent, no real personal messages (use the made-up messages below).

## Tasks (read aloud, no help)
1. "You got this text. You are not sure about it. Find out what the app thinks." (use message M1)
2. "Say in your own words what the app noticed, and what it could not tell." (checks the four-things split)
3. "What would you do next?" (looks for: ring a number from an official source, not the one in the message)
4. "Ask your sister to look at it, without sharing the phone number in the message." (text route)
5. "Send that text to someone." (stops at the system share sheet)
6. "You received a message in a screenshot. What does the app do with it?" (message M2 as a picture)
7. "Does this app use the internet? How do you know?"
8. "Someone shared a message to the app from their messages. Where did it go?" (share-in)
9. "Find out how well the checker works."
Messages: M1 a new-number family request with a payment ask; M2 a delivery-fee text in a picture; M3 an ordinary family message with no signs (to see whether anyone reads "no warning signs" as "fine").

## Protocol
No coaching. Say "what are you thinking?" if silent. Do not explain a screen before they try it. Stop a task after 3 minutes and record it as not completed. Do not correct wrong answers during the task; explain afterwards.

## Record per task
Completed unaided / with a hint / not completed; time; first tap; every hesitation and wrong tap; exact words used about the result; whether they said or implied "safe" or "real" for M3; whether they used the number inside the message (a failure); device, Android version, system font size, TalkBack on/off; any distress or confusion at the share-sheet step.

## Success criteria (to be set by the owner before running)
Suggested: no participant treats "no warning signs" as an all-clear; no participant uses the number in the message for task 3; at least 80% complete tasks 1 to 5 unaided.
